## Divide work by responsibility

A researcher gathers current options, a constraint solver checks dates and budgets, and an editor turns validated facts into a readable itinerary. The boundary is more valuable than adding agents.

## Preserve evidence

Each recommendation should retain its source, retrieval time, and relevant constraints. That makes the final answer inspectable and makes stale information easier to replace.
